Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

In short: everything you enter in GraftDay and every photo you take stays on your phone. Nothing is sent to us or to any other company. There is no account, no advertising and no usage tracking.

Who we are

GraftDay is developed by Erdem Kırcı. Contact: graftday.destek@gmail.com.

What you enter in the app

  • Procedure details: your first name, procedure date, technique, graft count and treated areas.
  • Clinic details: clinic name, phone and WhatsApp number.
  • Progress photos: photos you take in the app or pick from your library.
  • Care records: your care plan and the routines and tasks you complete.
  • Settings: your notification preferences and Face ID lock.

Some of this relates to your health and may count as special-category personal data under the GDPR and Turkish KVKK. Because it all stays on your device, we cannot access or process it.

Where your data is stored

  • All data is kept in the app's own storage on your phone. The app has no server.
  • Photos are stored with iOS file protection, so they cannot be read while your phone is locked. They are not saved to your photo library.
  • You can lock the app with Face ID. Face data is handled by Apple on your device; we never see it.
  • If iCloud Backup is on, iOS may include app data in your encrypted device backup. That backup lives in your Apple account and is covered by Apple's privacy terms.

Permissions

  • Camera: to take progress photos.
  • Photos: only when you pick a photo, to read that photo. We never access your whole library.
  • Notifications: for routine, photo-day and milestone reminders. They are created on your phone, not sent from a server.
  • Face ID: only if you turn on the app lock.

You can turn any permission off at any time in your phone's Settings.

Sharing

Your data is shared only when you choose to, for example when you send photos or a PDF report to your clinic via WhatsApp, email or another app. You start the share, and the privacy terms of the app you pick apply.

Payments

GraftDay PRO is purchased through Apple's App Store. We never see your payment details; Apple only tells the app whether your subscription is active. You can manage or cancel it in iPhone Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions.

Exporting and deleting your data

  • Export: Profile › Export photos gives you your photos or a PDF report.
  • Delete: Profile › Delete all data erases everything in the app. Deleting the app also removes all its data.

Since we hold none of your data, you don't need to contact us to export or delete it. For questions about your rights under the GDPR or KVKK, email graftday.destek@gmail.com.

Children

GraftDay is intended for users aged 18 and over.

Medical disclaimer

GraftDay is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. Information in the app is general; ask your clinic about your own situation. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Changes

If we change this policy we will publish the new version here and update the date above. If a change would send any data off your device (for example cloud backup), we will tell you in the app and ask for your consent first.
